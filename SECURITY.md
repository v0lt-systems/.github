# Security policy

This policy applies to every V0LT Systems repository that does not have its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately instead:

- on a public repository, with **Security → Report a vulnerability** (GitHub private vulnerability reporting);
- or by email to [vincent@v0lt.ai](mailto:vincent@v0lt.ai).

Include what is affected, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We acknowledge reports within a few working days and will tell you when a fix ships.
